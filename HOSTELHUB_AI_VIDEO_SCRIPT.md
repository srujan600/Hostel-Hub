## 💡 Quick Tips for Generating in Google Vids

1. **Set Up Your Slides / Scenes**:
   - In Google Vids, click **"Add Scene"** for each of the 18 scenes.
   - Set each scene's duration slider to **10.0 seconds**.
2. **Generate Real-Life Clips**:
   - For each scene, click **"Generate with AI"** or **"Add Media" > "Generate Video"**, paste the corresponding `Google Vids AI Prompt`, and select **"Photorealistic / Cinematic"**.
3. **Overlay Original Screenshots**:
   - In Google Vids, click **"Upload Media"**, choose the corresponding screenshot from `public/screenshots/`, and insert it over the video using **Picture-in-Picture**, **Split Screen**, or **Floating Frame**.
4. **Add Voiceover**:
   - In Google Vids, click **"Audio" > "Voiceover" > "Text-to-Speech"**, select the voice (e.g. *Cora* or *Alex*), and paste the 10-second VO snippet for each scene.
5. **Add Text & Captions**:
   - Copy-paste the `On-Screen Text Overlay` into the Google Vids Title/Lower-Third box styled with Collegiate Navy (`#00236f`) and clean white typography.
